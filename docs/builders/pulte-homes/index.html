Trent’s take

Trent's take

I sold for Pulte for six years, so I will be blunt: this is the value engine of the community. The Scenic series is the lowest entry into a detached home here, the Veranda and Front Porch series add the porches and the ADU option that make SeaFlower feel like SeaFlower, and the Distinctive series is where the bigger single-story plans live. Pulte's published "savings" on quick move-ins are a starting point, not a ceiling. Pulte moves most on price and incentives in the last two weeks of a quarter, and division leadership can approve things the on-site rep cannot ask for. Run the affiliated-lender math against an outside quote every time.

Pulte is the entry-price detached builder at SeaFlower: its $404,990 "from" price is the lowest single-family start in the community (only M/I's attached townhome product starts lower), spread across 105 homesites and the deepest plan menu after Cardel — 13 plans in four collections. It builds both front-load (Scenic, Distinctive) and alley-load/rear-garage (Veranda, Front Porch) single-family homes with Coastal and Farmhouse elevations, and it offers a dedicated ADU plan (Mabel II with ADU) — Cardel is the only other SeaFlower builder marketing a comparable option, via garage apartments/in-law suites. The trade-off is size: Pulte plans top out at 2,369 sq ft, the smallest ceiling among SeaFlower's detached builders, though block construction, tile roof, impact glass and a gas tankless water heater are standard.

Trent's take

Published incentives are the floor. The real conversation is about lot premium, closing-cost credits tied to the lender, design-center credits and structural options, and it happens with the sales manager, not the website. Timing matters: builders push hardest at the end of their fiscal quarters.

  • Tankless gas water heater and gas range
  • Smart Home prewire with CAT 6 and video doorbell

  • Sensa/Silestone counters, Aristokraft cabinets, Whirlpool appliances
  • HERS-rated energy efficiency

Watch-outs

  • HOA dues and any CDD are not published by Pulte; the "$899.34+/quarterly" figure comes from a third-party aggregator — confirm dues, CDD and any capital contribution with the sales team before contracting.
  • Builder Forward Commitment rate offers require financing through Pulte Mortgage and closing by a specified date; you are not required to use Pulte Mortgage to buy, and the advertised rates assume a 780 FICO.
  • All six quick move-ins are still under construction (anticipated completions Oct 2026–Jan 2027) — none is move-in ready — and Pulte's illustrative monthly payments exclude taxes, insurance and HOA dues.
  • Prices are moving: seaflower.com quoted a $399,990 "from" price in March 2026 versus $404,990 on pulte.com today, and three QMIs were just cut $10,000–$10,500 — re-check pulte.com before relying on any figure.
  • If you plan to rent the ADU on a Mabel II with ADU, the developer HOA requires one year of owner occupancy first and a six-month minimum lease.
