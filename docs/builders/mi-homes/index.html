Trent’s take

Trent's take

M/I is the entry ticket to SeaFlower and, right now, the builder with the most standing inventory. Townhomes and twin villas from $399,999, rear-load detached garages, and a long list of quick move-ins carrying "was" prices. Inventory carrying cost is leverage; a builder with twenty finished or nearly finished homes is a builder that negotiates. The attached-product HOA is a little higher because it covers exterior maintenance and insurance, which is exactly what a lock-and-leave buyer wants to pay for. Watch the advertised FHA rate offers: they are real, but they apply to specific homes and specific closing windows, and the APR is the number that matters.

M/I Homes is the only builder at SeaFlower selling attached, low-maintenance product — 90 villas and 68 townhomes on the community's narrowest homesites (22', 28' and 30') — and it carries the lowest published entry price in the community ("Priced from: $399,999" vs. Pulte's $399,990, David Weekley's mid-$400s bungalows, Cardel's mid-$500s and Issa's $1.3 million). Its villas are SeaFlower's only front-load-garage product, a departure from the community's rear-garage TND aesthetic, and elevations are limited to Coastal and West Indies. On 2026-09-03 it also had the most move-in-ready inventory of any SeaFlower builder observed: 21 quick move-in homes, six of them "Ready Now," most showing "was" markdowns of roughly $15k–$72k, backed by a choose-your-own incentive menu and an in-house FHA rate promotion through M/I Financial.

Trent's take

Published incentives are the floor. The real conversation is about lot premium, closing-cost credits tied to the lender, design-center credits and structural options, and it happens with the sales manager, not the website. Timing matters: builders push hardest at the end of their fiscal quarters.

Watch-outs

  • "Priced from: $399,999" is a quick move-in price; the lowest to-be-built plan starts at $404,999 (Ruby villa) and townhome plans at $431,999, and the October 2025 "high $300s" press-release pricing is stale.
  • The Summer Savings menu applies only to contracts written by September 15, 2026, the closing-cost and FHA-rate options require M/I Financial, and the offers cannot be combined.
  • HOA dues and any CDD are not published by M/I — the only figure is Jome's third-party "$264+/quarterly" — so confirm dues, what they cover, and any villa/townhome sub-associations from the HOA documents before contract.
  • "Was" markdowns of $15k–$72k on the 21 specs signal negotiable inventory but also volatility in comps and appraisals; six earlier listings had already disappeared (sold or under contract).
  • M/I's own pages disagree on square footage (Carmel 1,694 vs. 1,702; Bay Harbor 1,698 vs. 1,706), and the Sarasota division's Birdeye rating is 3.7 stars with workmanship and warranty complaints (none SeaFlower-specific).
