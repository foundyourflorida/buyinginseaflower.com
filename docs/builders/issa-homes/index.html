SeaFlower's only semi-custom builder, selling 80-foot-lot Estate Homes of 2,905–3,755 sq ft A/C (up to 4,523 with a garage apartment) in The Lakeshore and The Preserve, priced from $1.25M–$1.3M base plus homesite premium. Sales office: SeaFlower Welcome Center, 4505 Flower Fields Trail, Bradenton, FL 34210 (Hemingway model: 4515 Westlake Street, Bradenton, FL 34210). Verified 2026-09-03.

Trent’s take

Trent's take

Issa builds the estate tier: 80-foot lakefront lots on Lake Flores, semi-custom plans in the Lakeshore and Preserve collections, and a Hemingway model that won Best Overall at the 2026 Suncoast Parade of Homes. Read the pricing carefully. "From $1,250,000" excludes the homesite premium, and on a lakefront lot the premium is a real number that is only quoted on request. The two quick move-ins on Lake Flores Avenue are listed above $1.5 million. This is a longer build with a different deposit and draw structure than the production builders; get the contract reviewed before you sign it, not after.

Issa is SeaFlower's only seven-figure product and its only semi-custom builder: 80-foot Estate Home lots on Lake Flores (The Lakeshore) and preserve/lake-view lots (The Preserve), versus 45'–60' production lots, villas and townhomes from $399,999 to roughly $755,775 for every other builder. Buyers get a pre-construction plan-modification meeting, "Customized structural changes available (per builder's discretion)", designer-led selections, a framing walkthrough and an online progress portal instead of a fixed plan and design-studio option list. Architecture is "architecturally correct" West Indies, Coastal and Farmhouse with LRK Architects design, 12-foot or vaulted ceilings, 8-foot solid-core doors, GE Café or Bosch gas kitchens, impact glass, spray foam and whole-house dehumidification as standard. Sales run through Michael Saunders & Company (MLS-listed inventory, 3% buyer-broker commission) with a third-party RWC 1/2/10 warranty, and the Hemingway was the only SeaFlower model to take Best Overall in its 2026 Parade of Homes category.

Before your first visit

If you plan to visit a model, tell me first. Builders register the agent who introduces you, and some will not let you add representation after a first unaccompanied visit. A five-minute call protects your options; it costs nothing.

Incentives and promotions

  • No incentive is currently published by the builder for SeaFlower. That does not mean nothing is available; it means it is negotiated on site.
Trent's take

Published incentives are the floor. The real conversation is about lot premium, closing-cost credits tied to the lender, design-center credits and structural options, and it happens with the sales manager, not the website. Timing matters: builders push hardest at the end of their fiscal quarters.

What stands out

  • Impact-rated windows and doors (PGT per the Preserve post)
  • Vaulted or 12-foot ceilings in living areas; 8-foot solid-core doors
  • GE Café or Bosch appliances with 36" gas cooktop
  • 42" upper cabinets, crown molding, dovetail drawers, plywood box construction
  • Kohler plumbing fixtures; on-demand natural gas water heater
  • Spray foam insulation and whole-house dehumidification
  • Oversized garages with golf-cart storage; paver driveways and covered rear porch
  • "Customized structural changes available (per builder's discretion)"

Watch-outs

  • Three different "from" prices are live at once: the microsite headers say "starting from $1,250,000", the estate-homes body says "base price starts at $1.3 million", the features page says "$1.29 million", and the Parade listing says "From the Low $1,300,000s" — all excluding homesite premium.
  • Homesite premiums are excluded from every quoted price and are disclosed only on request (the Jan 2026 "Premiums Brochure" is an image-only site map with no dollar figures).
  • Spec pricing has moved fast: 7808 Lake Flores Ave (Amelia) was raised to $1,390,000 on Apr 6 2026 (+3.0%, implying about $1,350,000 at its Mar 11 listing) and to $1,567,560 on Aug 10 2026 (+12.77%), roughly 16% in five months; both specs sit well above the advertised base.
  • Lake Flores CDD applies to Issa's 80-foot lots even though Jome shows $0: about $2,978–$3,409 per year under the FY2027 proposed budget, and up to $6,332 per year where Series 2023A-2 debt was not prepaid at closing — confirm which tier applies to your lot.
  • Plan lineup is in flux: Kingston is named in the intro text but has no plan card; Palm Beach and Sanibel are "Coming Soon" with no specs; Hatteras and the Hemingway 3-car share identical square footage with an unexplained relationship.
