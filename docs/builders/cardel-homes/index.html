Cardel Homes at SeaFlower: plans, prices and what to know before you sign

Cardel Homes at SeaFlower:

Cottage (50') and Classic (60') single-family homes in both front-garage and alley-garage versions, with natural gas, whole-house impact glass and block construction standard and an optional above-garage apartment on most rear-garage plans. From the $500s. 19 floor plans on 50', 60' lots, 1,900–3,172 sq ft, 3–5 bedrooms. Sales office: 4521 Flower Fields Trail (Daybreak model), Bradenton, FL 34210. Verified 2026-09-03.

Trent’s take

Trent's take

Cardel is the design-forward builder here, and the one that took the coastal-village brief most literally: front-garage and rear-garage versions of most plans, natural gas, and garage suites that add roughly 800 square feet on many models. I walked every Cardel model on camera; realistically you are shopping $700,000 to $1.2 million once you add the lot and the options people actually pick, even though base prices start lower. Cardel runs design-option promotions in cash amounts rather than rate buydowns, which suits buyers paying cash or bringing their own lender. Build times are longer than the production builders, so ask for the construction schedule in writing.

Cardel is SeaFlower's mid-to-upper single-family builder: base prices of $579,990–$811,990 sit above M/I and Pulte, overlap David Weekley, and land just under Issa's $1.3M+ estates. It is the builder offering rear (alley)-garage plans at both 50' and 60' widths, and its 3-car rear garages and '2.0' two-story variants are the community's larger-footprint TND product. Its standard spec bundles natural gas, whole-house impact windows, block-with-poured-cells construction and 8' interior doors, items often optional elsewhere. Most distinctive is the ADU menu: ten plans with an optional above-garage apartment plus Palma's first-floor suite, the widest garage-apartment lineup among SeaFlower's five builders.

Trent's take

Published incentives are the floor. The real conversation is about lot premium, closing-cost credits tied to the lender, design-center credits and structural options, and it happens with the sales manager, not the website. Timing matters: builders push hardest at the end of their fiscal quarters.

What stands out

  • Natural gas standard: 30" gas range and tankless gas water heater
  • Whole-house impact-resistant windows
  • Concrete block with poured, steel-reinforced cells and hurricane-strapped trusses
  • Optional above-garage apartment (+800 sq ft where shown) on rear-garage plans
  • Front- or alley-load garages at both 50' and 60' widths; 3-car option on rear plans
  • 42" Level 3 maple cabinets, Whirlpool stainless appliances, Moen Brantford faucets
  • Raised 8' interior doors, Cat 6 wiring, 200-amp service
  • Craftsman, Coastal and Transitional Farmhouse elevations; pool-ready homesites

Watch-outs

  • Three different incentive statements appear on Cardel's own pages ($25K flex dollars on the QMI page, $20K toward design options on the community page, 'contact a consultant' on the promos page); the $25K expires Sept 30, 2026 and cannot be combined, and the rate buydown is explicitly not offered at SeaFlower. Pin down in writing which applies to a to-be-built contract.
  • Base prices exclude lot premiums, options and design-center selections; Cardel says buyers typically spend 10–20% of base on selections, and the two Coral specs are priced roughly $125K–$140K above the $598,990 Coral base.
  • Cardel never labels plans as one- or two-story; story counts here are inferred from floor-plan tabs, and Tradewind, Sunrise and Cove 2.0 are the least certain. Cove 2.0 carries the garage-apartment badge on the list page but no suite option on its plan page.
  • Annual CDD assessments for 50'/60' lots range from about $2,011 to $4,850 depending on phase and bond series, and which Lake Flores CDD phase covers Cardel's streets is unverified; ask for the exact lot's assessment before contract.
  • The ADU is not a short-term rental play: developer rules require one year of owner-occupancy before renting and a six-month minimum lease, and Bora, Sea Breeze and Tradewind publish no square footage for their optional suite.
